# Day 6 Quiz
## 1. What does range(2, 10, 2)
Answer: 2,4,6,8
## 2. What is the difference between break and continue?
Answer: "break" stops the whole loop while "continue" skips the current iteration and moves to the next iteration.
## 3. When does a loop's else clause execute?
The else clause executes when the loop finishes normally without being stopped by a break.
